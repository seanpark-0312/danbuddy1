# AI practice buddy video clips

Put the clips for the AI buddy's video call here. The chat page plays them automatically when they exist
and shows an animated placeholder when they do not.

    emma/hello.mp4   played once when the call connects (smiles and waves)
    emma/idle.mp4    loops while listening (nods, blinks)
    emma/talk.mp4    loops while the buddy's reply is shown and spoken
    emma/still.jpg   used instead when there are no clips: one photo with a gentle breathing motion
    minjun/...       same three files for Minjun (optional)

MP4 (H.264), 16:9, 720p is enough, no audio needed, ideally under 5 MB each.
